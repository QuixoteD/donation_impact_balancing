# GISI allocation dashboard: provenance and decisions

Working record for `gisi_allocation_dashboard.html`. Kept out of the page source from v06 so that nothing internal ships to donors who view source.

## Version history

- v02 to v03, 17/11/2026: evidence re-sourced to the closest literature for each part (referral evidence from child protection programmes with referral as a core output).
- v04, 17/11/2026: "How to read the numbers" and the unit-cost editor replaced by a short disclaimer; GISI recommended mix set as the opening allocation.
- v05, 17/11/2026: pupils reached per school and per teacher from the 2025 Annual School Census national averages (MBSSE, 2025).
- v06, 17/11/2026: pre-publication review (see the review section below).

## User decisions, 17/11/2026

- All nine GISI elements were delivered in GISI1 or GISI2.
- Four menu items added: school infrastructure, menstrual kits, community baseline survey, teacher rural retention.
- Payroll cleaning keeps its own slider. It covers verification of staff during digitisation (Wi De Ya takes the payroll list to the school; corrections go to the ministry), support to the payroll steering committee, government reporting and processes, and district staff verification visits.
- Referral support is counted per child linked to a service (boys and girls).
- Comparator costs stay until Corus supplies its own.
- Recommended mix (v04): cash transfer 50%; digitising schools 10%; case management 10%; training 10% (school management 5%, local government capacity building 5%); referral, wrap-around training, targeting and payroll cleaning 5% each; menstrual kits, school infrastructure, baseline survey and rural retention 0%.

## Provenance of costs

- Corus programme menu, 9/11/2026: cash US$29 per girl per month incl. overheads; home visit US$30; school leadership training US$500 per school; digitising US$750 per school; sessions US$110 (menstrual health) + US$110 (child rights, upper value) + US$268 (positive parenting) per school; menstrual kit US$11 per girl (three-year life); school infrastructure from US$5,000 per school; community baseline US$770 per school.
- Teacher rural retention: US$100 per teacher per year with fewer than six unauthorised absences (user, 17/11/2026).
- Comparators: targeting under US$3 per learner (Haimovich, Vazquez and Adelman, 2021); local government 3.6 US cents per extra dollar delivered (Muralidharan et al., 2021); payroll about US$51 per teacher, [Maths by author] US$1 million grant / about 19,500 teachers (Ladhani and Sinclair, 2018); referral US$9.77 per beneficiary per year, OVC case management with home visits and referrals in South Africa (MEASURE Evaluation, 2019).
- Pupils per school 3,450,104 / 13,444 = 256.6 and girls per school 1,781,249 / 13,444 = 132.5 are [Maths by author] from the 2025 Annual School Census; 37 pupils per teacher is stated in the census.
- "Why it matters" text is Corus's programme view.
